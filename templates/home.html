Gran Turismo 7 is a premier racing simulator developed by Polyphony Digital for PlayStation. Combining realistic driving physics with stunning graphics, it features hundreds of detailed cars, dynamic weather, deep customization, and iconic tracks. Players can collect, tune, and race vehicles across offline career campaigns and competitive online modes.

Yas Marina Circuit in Gran Turismo 7 delivers high-speed Abu Dhabi racing on the modified layout. Stripping away former chicanes, it offers longer straights, open hairpin turns, and smooth sweepers around the hotel. It's a technical track demanding precise braking, high downforce tuning, and careful throttle control under bright trackside lights.

Gran Turismo 7 esports teams feature elite sim racers competing in high-stakes, official global competitions like the GT World Series. Drivers represent top automotive manufacturers or their home nations. Demanding pixel-perfect precision and strategy, these teams battle for prestige on real-world tracks in ultra-competitive, broadcasted events.
